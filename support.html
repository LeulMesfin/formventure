Support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Write to us and we'll reply by email.

formventure.support@gmail.com

In the app you can also use You → My Account → Feedback & Help. It opens your email app with your message and the app version already filled in.

Delete your profile and data

FormVenture has no sign-in: everything you save lives in a profile on your phone.

  1. Open FormVenture and go to You.
  2. Tap My Account, then Privacy & security.
  3. Tap Delete profile, then Delete profile again to confirm.

This permanently removes the profile and its plans, workouts, food diary, progress and cycle records from your phone. Other profiles on the same phone are kept. FormVenture has no server copy to delete. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may still hold older copies until it is replaced. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the phone.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Go to You → My Account → Privacy & security → Export my data and share the copy wherever you like.

Do I need an account or password?

No. FormVenture asks only for your first name. To keep it private, turn on App lock in You → My Account → Privacy & security: it opens with Face ID, your fingerprint or your phone's passcode.

Several people use one phone

Each person can have their own profile with their own plan and progress. Go to You → My Account → Switch profile, then tap New profile.

Moving to a new phone

Your data stays on the phone it was saved on. Use Export my data to keep your own copy. Moving data between phones is planned for a future version.

Is my data backed up?

FormVenture keeps your data on your phone only. Your phone's own iCloud or Google backup may include it. Use Export my data for your own copy.

A barcode isn't found

Product details come from the free Open Food Facts database, which doesn't have every product. Tap Add Food and enter the details from the label instead.

Reminders don't show up

Go to You → My Account → Privacy & security and check that Notifications says Allowed. Tap it to change it in your phone's Settings.

Is this medical advice?

No. FormVenture gives general fitness and nutrition guidance. Talk to a doctor before big changes to diet or exercise.
