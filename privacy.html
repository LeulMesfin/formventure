Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Short version: what you enter in FormVenture stays on your phone. We show no ads, use no tracking, and never sell or share your data.

What FormVenture saves

Your profile, plan, workouts, food diary, water, weight, measurements and cycle records. They are saved only on your phone, in your profile. FormVenture has no sign-in and no server that receives them. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include them.

What leaves your phone

When you scan or type a barcode, only that barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product. Nothing else you enter is sent anywhere.

Camera, photos and notifications

The camera is used only to scan food barcodes. A profile photo you choose stays on your phone. Notifications are only the reminders and timers you turn on. Face ID or fingerprint unlock, if you turn it on, is handled by your phone; FormVenture never sees your face or fingerprint.

No ads or tracking

FormVenture shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares your data.

Your choices

You can export your data or delete your profile at any time in You → My Account → Privacy & security. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone. See how to delete your profile.

Health information

FormVenture gives general fitness and nutrition guidance. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before big changes to diet or exercise.

Children

FormVenture is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new date.

Questions

Email us at formventure.support@gmail.com.
